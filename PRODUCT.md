# PRODUCT.md — Aptely

## Product Purpose
Aptely is a B2B SaaS recruitment intelligence platform. It helps tech recruiters move beyond CVs by verifying candidate skills, assessing soft skills, and ranking candidates against a role's DNA — in hours, not weeks. The goal: evidence-based hiring decisions, not gut-feel.

## Users
- **Primary:** Tech recruiters, HR leads, hiring managers at SMBs and scale-ups (5–500 employees). French-speaking market first (EU expansion).
- **Secondary:** Candidates who build verified "Capability Records" — a richer alternative to a CV.

## Register
**Brand** — the landing page is a marketing surface where design IS the product signal. It must communicate credibility, precision, and differentiation from generic AI hiring tools.

## Tone
Direct, confident, honest. No hype language ("seamless", "elevate", "revolutionize"). Aptely admits its limitations because honesty builds trust with skeptical tech recruiters. Think: a senior engineer explaining the product, not a marketing person selling it.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI SaaS landing pages (purple gradients on white, orbs, hero-metrics grids)
- Job board aesthetics (LinkedIn, Indeed)
- "Future of work" consultancy decks
- Notion-style minimalism (too developer-y, not HR enough)

## Strategic Principles
- Evidence > Claims. Show the product interface, not abstract icons.
- Precision signals trust. Every pixel communicates rigor.
- Dark = brand. The landing stays dark even when the app is light.
- No decoration without purpose. Orbs, blurs, gradients must earn their place.

## Color
Primary accent: oklch(65% 0.22 42) — Aptely orange (#FF6B00). Used sparingly (buttons, key highlights). Not on large surfaces.
Background dark: oklch(14% 0.025 255) — near-black with cool blue undertone.

## Typography
Display: Cabinet Grotesk — landing headlines only.
Body: System UI / sans-serif — clean, fast, readable.

## Competition
Workable, Lever, Greenhouse (process tools), HireSweet (sourcing), Eightfold (AI matching) — none focus on verified skill evidence as the core differentiator.
